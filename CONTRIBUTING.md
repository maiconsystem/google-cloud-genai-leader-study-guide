# Contributing

Short version: **issues yes, pull requests no.**

---

## Why this repo is read-only

This is a record of how one person prepared for and passed the Google Cloud Generative AI Leader exam, kept accurate and consistent by one maintainer. It is not a community wiki. Keeping write access closed is what lets a reader trust that everything here was actually used, verified, and believed correct by someone who sat the exam.

So: **pull requests are not accepted, and write access is not granted.** Please do not take it personally — it is about provenance, not about your change.

---

## What is genuinely useful

**Open an issue** for any of these, and I will make the edit:

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔄 **A product rename** | The most valuable contribution. Google renames things constantly. See [docs/04-product-naming-2026.md](docs/04-product-naming-2026.md) |
| ❌ **A factual error** | Something here is wrong, or has stopped being true |
| 🔗 **A dead link** | Google moves URLs often |
| 📝 **A change to the exam** | New weights, new format, new price, a new domain |
| 💡 **A resource that helped you** | Especially free ones, and especially anything using the current vocabulary |
| ❓ **Something unclear** | If an explanation did not land for you, it is not landing for others either |

**Please include a source** where you can — a link to Google's documentation, the current exam guide, or the certification page. That is what lets me verify it quickly rather than guess.

---

## What will be declined

- **Real exam questions, "dumps", or anything from a brain-dump site.** They violate Google's exam agreement, can get a candidate's certification revoked, and are not needed to pass. This repository will not host them, and issues containing them will be closed and deleted.
- **Requests to confirm whether a specific question is "on the exam."** I will not discuss actual exam content. Neither should you — you agreed not to when you registered.
- Large structural rewrites, or additions that turn this into a general gen AI resource. The scope is deliberately one exam.

---

## Forking

Go ahead. The [licence](LICENSE) is CC BY 4.0 — copy it, adapt it, translate it, teach from it. Just credit the source and link back.

If you are translating it into another language, I would genuinely like to know: open an issue and I will link to your fork from the README.
