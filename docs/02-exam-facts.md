# Exam facts

Everything logistical, in one place. Confirmed against Google's certification page in September 2026 — **re-check before you book**, because prices and languages change.

---

## The exam

| | |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Google Cloud Certified — Generative AI Leader |
| **Level** | Foundational |
| **Questions** | **50–60 multiple choice and multiple select** |
| **Duration** | **90 minutes** (≈95 seconds per question) |
| **Cost** | **$99 USD** plus tax |
| **Format** | Online-proctored (Pearson VUE OnVUE) or onsite at a test centre |
| **Prerequisites** | **None** |
| **Valid for** | **3 years** |
| **Languages** | English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese |
| **Result** | Provisional pass/fail on screen immediately; official confirmation by email, typically within a few days |
| **Passing score** | **Not published by Google.** Aim for 80%+ on practice material and stop worrying about it |
| **Retake policy** | 14 days after a first failure, 60 days after a second, 365 days after a third |

## The four domains

| Domain | Weight | What it really tests |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Fundamentals of gen AI** | ~30% | Vocabulary and concepts. AI/ML/gen AI hierarchy, learning approaches, foundation models, the five-layer stack, data quality and types |
| **2. Google Cloud's gen AI offerings** | **~35%** | **Which product solves this problem.** The largest domain and mostly recall |
| **3. Techniques to improve model output** | ~20% | RAG vs fine-tuning, prompting techniques, parameters, grounding, human-in-the-loop |
| **4. Business strategies** | ~15% | Value, adoption, risk, security, responsible AI, the gen AI adoption lifecycle |

*Google publishes the domains; the percentages are the commonly cited weights and match how the exam reads in practice. The authoritative topic list is the [official exam guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_exam_guide_english.pdf).*

## Who it is for

Google's own description of the certified individual:

> *"A visionary professional with comprehensive knowledge of how gen AI can transform and be used within a business… business-level knowledge of Google Cloud's gen AI products and services… **Their expertise is in strategic leadership and influence, not technical implementation**, though they have a conceptual understanding of gen AI concepts and technology."*

**Read the bold part twice — it tells you what the wrong answers look like.** When two options are both defensible, the exam nearly always wants the one a leader would choose: the efficient, governed, business-aligned option, not the technically maximal one.

There is **no coding and no console work.** A non-technical product manager can pass this. An engineer can fail it by over-engineering the answers.

## Booking it

1. Create an account at [Google Cloud certification registration](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/register) (CertMetrics).
2. Choose **online-proctored** or a test centre.
3. Pay $99 plus local tax.
4. **If online: run the [OnVUE system test](https://home.pearsonvue.com/onvue) on the exact machine, network and room you will use.** A failed check on the day costs you the slot and the fee.

**On the day, for an online exam:**

- Check-in usually opens ~30 minutes early. Be at the desk early; the check-in itself takes 10–15 minutes.
- Government photo ID, matching the name on your registration.
- Clear desk, clear walls, no second screen, no phone within reach, no one else in the room.
- No scratch paper unless the proctor explicitly permits it.

---

## After you pass

- A **digital badge** (Credly) and a certificate, issued within a few days.
- **Valid 3 years.** Renewal is a shorter renewal exam that opens before expiry — not a full re-sit.
- Google's certification benefits (store credit, perks) vary by year and level; check the certification page for what applies to foundational certs at the time you pass.
