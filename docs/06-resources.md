# Resources — every link worth having

Grouped by how useful each one was, not by source.

## 1. Official Google material (start here, it is free)

| Resource | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **[Certification page](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/generative-ai-leader)** | The authoritative page. Registration, price, languages, validity. **Check it before you book** — it is also where Google announced the 2026 product renaming |
| **[Official exam guide (PDF)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_exam_guide_english.pdf)** | The list of every topic that can be examined, in Google's own words. **If anything in this repo disagrees with it, the guide wins.** Read it twice: once at the start to scope your study, once at the end as a checklist |
| **[Official sample questions](https://forms.gle/soztS7Q74AXBncATA)** | Free Google Form, closest thing to the real exam that exists publicly. **The feedback text after each answer is the real value** — it contains Google's own one-line definitions of each product. Do this last, as a final check |
| **[Google Skills — "Generative AI Leader Certification" path (path 1951)](https://www.skills.google/paths/1951)** | **I used this one.** Google's own learning path for this exam: 5 activities, on-demand courses covering gen AI fundamentals plus Gemini Advanced, Gemini Notebook and Google AI Studio. **The best free source for Domain 2**, and the only course material written in the current product vocabulary. Formerly branded Google Cloud Skills Boost — [the old URL](https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/paths/1951) still resolves |
| **[Google Cloud product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/)** | For looking up a single product when a definition will not stick |

## 2. YouTube

These are the videos I actually used. The first is the most valuable if you only watch one.

| Video | Why watch it |
|---|---|
| **[Google Cloud Generative AI Leader 2026: 112 Practice Exam Questions & Answers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dc2a-7uyWhA)** | A large bank of practice questions read out with answers. Good for passive revision — play it while walking. **Verify the product names against the 2026 renaming**, as some question banks still use the old ones. The link starts at 19:15 in the original share: [jump there](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dc2a-7uyWhA&t=1155s) |
| **[Foundations of gen AI — Intro](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ahQHKLBsE40)** | Google's own framing of Domain 1. Short, and it uses the vocabulary the exam uses |

## 3. Paid video courses

Optional — everything examinable is available free above. I used Pluralsight because I already had access through an employer.

See **[../courses/01-genai-leader-path.md](../courses/01-genai-leader-path.md)** for a course-by-course summary of what each one covers, so you can decide whether it is worth your hours.

| Course | Notes |
|---|---|
| Pluralsight — "Google Cloud Generative AI Leader" path (4 courses, ~6h) | Titles map 1:1 onto the four exam domains. Uses the **old product names** throughout |
| Pluralsight — Practice Exam (50 questions, 90 min) | The single most useful item on Pluralsight. Scores you by domain |
| Coursera / DeepLearning.AI — "Generative AI for Everyone" | Free to audit. Below the level of this exam, but good if gen AI is new to you |

## 4. Practice questions

| Source | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **[Google's official sample questions](https://forms.gle/soztS7Q74AXBncATA)** | Essential. Free |
| **[practice/01-practice-questions.md](../practice/01-practice-questions.md)** in this repo | 60 original questions with full explanations, weighted like the exam |
| **[practice/02-domain-2-product-drill.md](../practice/02-domain-2-product-drill.md)** | 40 scenario-to-product questions in the current vocabulary |
| Pluralsight practice exam | Worth it if you have access. Mine was dated December 2025 and used the old product names |
| ExamTopics and similar community sites | Use with care. Answers are often wrong and the comments disagree with each other. Never treat them as authoritative, and never use a site that claims to have real exam items |

> **On dumps:** sites advertising "real exam questions" violate Google's exam agreement and can get your certification revoked. They are also unnecessary — this exam rewards understanding the product catalogue, and that is learnable in a weekend.
