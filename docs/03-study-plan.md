# Study plan

Three schedules. Pick the one that matches your time, not your ambition.

The plan I actually followed is the three-week one, at about 4 hours a day — and roughly a third of those hours were wasted on material that was not examined. The schedules below are corrected for that.

---

## Plan A — Three weeks, ~2 hours a day (≈40 hours)

For someone new to generative AI, or wanting comfortable margin.

### Week 1 — Foundations

| Day | ~2 hrs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Exam facts](02-exam-facts.md) · [the 2026 renaming](04-product-naming-2026.md) · skim the [official exam guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_exam_guide_english.pdf) | You know the shape of the exam and the correct product vocabulary |
| 2–3 | [Google Skills path 1951](https://www.skills.google/paths/1951) | Google's own framing, in current names |
| 4–5 | [Study guide](../study-guide/01-full-study-guide.md) Sections 1–2 (technique + Domain 1) | Fundamentals done |
| 6 | [Study guide](../study-guide/01-full-study-guide.md) Section 3 (Domain 2) — **slowly, with notes** | The biggest domain, first pass |
| 7 | **Practice exam under real conditions. Score by domain.** | Four numbers that direct weeks 2 and 3 |

> **Take a practice exam at the end of week 1, not week 3.** You get one clean diagnostic run before you start remembering answers, and everything after it is targeted.

## Rules that matter more than the schedule

1. **Score every practice attempt by domain.** An overall percentage tells you nothing you can act on. Four numbers tell you exactly what to do next.
2. **Give Domain 2 more time than any course gives it.** It is ~35% of the exam; course paths typically give it under 20% of their runtime. That imbalance is the single biggest structural trap in preparing for this exam.
3. **Do not start new material in the last 48 hours.** Recognition-heavy exams reward rest over cramming.
4. **Book the date before you feel ready.** An unbooked exam expands to fill all available months.
5. **Re-check the product names the week you sit.** Google renames things faster than any study material can track. Ten minutes with the [official exam guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/generative_ai_leader_exam_guide_english.pdf).

## My actual numbers, for calibration

| | |
|---|---|
| Video courses watched | ~6 hours (Pluralsight path) + Google Skills path |
| First practice exam | **39/50 = 78%** — Fundamentals 93%, Techniques 80%, Offerings 71%, Business 63% |
| Time between that and the exam | 7 days, almost all spent on Domains 2 and 4 |
| Google's official sample questions | **100%** |
| Real exam | **Passed, first attempt** |

The 78% → pass gap was closed almost entirely by the [Domain 2 drill](../practice/02-domain-2-product-drill.md), the [product table](../study-guide/03-product-table.md) and the four habits in [common mistakes](../practice/03-common-mistakes.md). Not by more video.
